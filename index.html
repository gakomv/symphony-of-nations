What is Model United Nations?

Model United Nations (MUN) is an educational simulation where participants step into the roles of diplomats representing member states of the United Nations. Delegates research global issues, debate international policy, draft resolutions, and negotiate with peers to address the world's most pressing challenges.

MUN cultivates critical thinking, public speaking, research skills, and cross-cultural understanding. It transforms young leaders into informed global citizens capable of navigating complex diplomatic landscapes.

Committees

General Assembly (GA)
Classic

The General Assembly is the UN's most representative body, where delegates discuss global political, economic, humanitarian, and social issues. Representing the positions of their respective countries, delegates participate in debates, draft resolutions, negotiate with other delegations, and work toward consensus on the world's most pressing international challenges.

Security Council (UNSC)
Classic

The United Nations Security Council is responsible for maintaining international peace and security. Delegates address armed conflicts, threats to peace, terrorism, and international crises. The Council has the authority to adopt legally binding resolutions, including sanctions, peacekeeping operations, and other measures aimed at restoring stability.

Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC)
Classic

The Economic and Social Council serves as a platform for discussing sustainable development, the global economy, education, public health, technological innovation, and poverty reduction. Delegates collaborate to develop long-term solutions to the world's most significant social and economic challenges.

Human Rights Council (UNHRC)
Classic

The Human Rights Council focuses on the promotion and protection of human rights worldwide. Delegates examine human rights violations, discuss humanitarian crises, and propose mechanisms for international cooperation to prevent abuses and strengthen the protection of fundamental freedoms.

Digital Governance Council
New

The Digital Governance Council is a forward-looking committee dedicated to the governance of the digital world. Delegates debate issues such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data protection, digital sovereignty, combating disinformation, and the development of international norms for emerging technologies. The committee's primary objective is to strike a balance between innovation, security, and human rights.

Board of Global CEOs
New

The Board of Global CEOs is an innovative committee in which delegates represent leading multinational corporations rather than sovereign states. Participants make high-level strategic business decisions, respond to global crises, foster international cooperation, and address issues including investment, sustainable development, technological innovation, and corporate responsibility. This committee combines diplomacy, business strategy, negotiation, and crisis management in a dynamic decision-making environment.

International Court of Justice (ICJ)
New

The International Court of Justice (ICJ) is the principal judicial organ of the United Nations, responsible for resolving legal disputes between states under international law. Delegates assume the roles of legal representatives, judges, or legal advisers, examining evidence, interpreting international legal principles, and presenting well-reasoned legal arguments. This committee emphasizes rigorous legal analysis, persuasive advocacy, and judicial decision-making.
